# User guide

For students and teachers. No code required.

---

## What SkillMap AI does

It shows you the distance between the skills you have and the skills a career
requires, then gives you a plan to close that distance — and updates the plan
as you make progress.

It will **not** tell you a salary, promise you a job, or give you a percentage
chance of being hired. Those numbers would be guesses dressed as facts, and
they change anyway.

---

## Getting started

### 1. Create an account

You need a name, email, password, university, department, and academic year.

**Already have an account?** Sign in on the login page. A demo student is
seeded for trying things out.

### 2. The setup wizard

Five short steps — Profile, Skills, CV, Career, Done.

Every step can be skipped, and you can come back later from your profile page.
The wizard remembers where you stopped, so you can close the tab and pick up
where you left off.

**A gap is better than nothing.** Only add skills you have actually used, even
at level 1. A skill you tried once is real, and it changes your map. An
inflated map produces a plan that does not help you.

### 3. Choose a target career

Either browse the list, or take the **career quiz** — ten questions, about two
minutes, no wrong answers. It suggests three careers with reasons.

You can change your target at any time. Nothing is lost.

## Recording your skills honestly

Each skill is rated **0 to 5**:

| Level | Meaning                             |
| ----- | ----------------------------------- |
| 0     | Never used this                     |
| 1     | Tried it once or twice with help    |
| 2     | Can do simple guided tasks          |
| 3     | Can complete tasks independently    |
| 4     | Can handle complex real-world tasks |
| 5     | Can teach others and advise on it   |

**Where a number came from is always shown:**

| Badge                        | Meaning                                     |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| You added                    | You entered it yourself                     |
| AI extracted — review needed | Suggested from your CV. Check it.           |
| Quiz verified                | Confirmed by a mini skill check             |
| Completed via roadmap        | You finished the sessions for it            |
| Completed via project        | A project raised it, with your confirmation |

### Uploading a CV

PDF or DOCX, up to 5 MB.

You will see the original text on the left and everything found on the right.
**Nothing is added to your profile until you accept it.** For each item you can
accept it, edit it, or reject it. Items the extractor was unsure about are
flagged — read those carefully.

Your CV is private. There is no link anyone can guess to read it, and you can
delete it at any time.

## Reading your results

### Alignment percentage

How much of a career's required skills you currently have, with
high-importance skills counting for more.

> It measures alignment with a list of skills. It is not a prediction of
> employment, and no tool can honestly make that prediction.

### Your skill gap

For every required skill:

| Label          | Meaning                                               |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Strong**     | You are at or above the required level                |
| **Developing** | One level away                                        |
| **Gap**        | Two levels away, lower importance                     |
| **Critical**   | Three or more away, or two on a high-importance skill |

Every label has a colour **and** a word **and** an icon, so it never depends on
seeing colour.

**"Why?"** on any number shows the calculation using your own numbers.

### Your learning order

Ranked by five things: how big the gap is, how much the career says the skill
matters, what depends on it, how long it takes, and how close it is to what you
already know.

**Prerequisites always come first.** If SQL is needed before Power BI, SQL is
taught first, even if Power BI would score higher on the other factors.

## Your roadmap

Generated from your actual gaps, paced to your weekly study hours. Change your
study hours in Settings and re-plan.

**Three views** of the same plan:

- **Timeline** — month by month
- **Weekly checklist** — tick items off
- **Kanban** — To do, In progress, Done

**"Why this order?"** on every item.

**Marking something done** can raise a skill level — but only if you confirm
the new level. SkillMap never raises a level on its own.

### When the plan changes

Finish something and the roadmap adapts. It creates a new version and tells you
why, in plain language:

> "You completed SQL, so Statistics moved to Month 1."

**Nothing you have finished is ever lost.** Completed work carries forward.

## Resources and projects

**Resources** are free-first. Filter by skill, level, type, cost, and
language. A resource marked _sample_ has not had its link verified, so treat it
with care.

**Projects** are the fastest way to make a skill stick. Each has a step
checklist. Finishing one can raise the skills it practises — again, only with
your confirmation.

## Tracking progress

- **Log a study session.** Fifteen minutes counts.
- **Weekly goal ring** — progress toward your study-hours target.
- **Streak** — consecutive days with a session. A missed day does not erase it;
  it just ends the current run, which is the honest reading.
- **Achievements** — sixteen, awarded from what you actually recorded.
- **Alignment trend** — a snapshot whenever your skills or career change, so
  you can see whether you are actually moving.

## The assistant

A button in the corner of every page.

It answers from **your** data and labels where each answer came from:

- **From your profile** — your recorded skills, gaps, or study hours
- **From career database** — what a role requires
- **AI suggestion** — general guidance with no specific data behind it

**Try asking:** "What should I learn next?" · "How close am I to Data Analyst?" ·
"Why is SQL important?" · "Suggest a project"

**It will not** quote a salary, promise you a job, or give a hiring percentage.
Asking for a salary gets a polite refusal, because any figure it could give you
would be misleading.

## Accessibility and low-data settings

In **Settings** you can change:

| Setting           | What it does                                                             |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Language**      | English or বাংলা                                                         |
| **Theme**         | Light, dark, or match your device                                        |
| **Text size**     | 85% to 150%                                                              |
| **Low-data mode** | Turns off animations and replaces the skill map graph with a simple list |

Low-data mode is for slow or expensive connections. It removes the animation
and the interactive graph, and **nothing is lost** — the same information is
laid out more cheaply.

The whole app also works with a keyboard, has visible focus, and works with a
screen reader. Every chart has a table equivalent.

## Your data and your privacy

- Your CV is never public.
- Nothing from a CV reaches your profile without your confirmation.
- Age, gender, religion, and similar are never collected, inferred, or used.
- Your data is not sold or shared for advertising.
- **Delete your account any time** from Profile → Danger zone. It removes your
  profile, skills, CV and its file, roadmaps, progress, and achievements. It
  cannot be undone.

Full detail in the [privacy notice](/privacy) and
[`docs/AI_MODEL.md`](AI_MODEL.md).

## Common questions

**My score went down. Did I break something?**
No. Alignment is a measurement, not a reward. Adding a higher-priority skill can
change the total even while you improve overall — the trend line shows this
better than the headline number.

**A level looks wrong.**
Change it. Your self-rating is the input, and a wrong input gives a wrong plan.

**Should I do a skill I have no gap in?**
Your call. The plan optimises for the biggest gaps, but a skill at level 3 that
a role wants at 5 is still worth your time if you enjoy it.

**Can I use this without a CV?**
Yes. Add skills by hand. The CV is a convenience, not a requirement.

**Does it work offline?**
In Demo Mode, yes, apart from the pages that need the database.

**Is my data used to train a model?**
No. See [the AI page](/ai-info) for exactly what leaves your machine and what
does not.
